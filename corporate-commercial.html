Focus area

Corporate & Commercial

The business-facing core of the legal practice: transactions, contracts, governance and the disputes that follow them. Written so the person responsible for the decision can act on it.

Overview

The legal work that runs the business

Corporate and commercial instructions rarely arrive in isolation. A funding round raises governance questions. A new supplier arrangement raises liability questions. A margin squeeze raises contract and pricing questions. We take the whole picture rather than the single document that was sent to us.

Our drafting is deliberately practical. We allocate risk where the money and the exposure actually sit, we avoid provisions that are impossible to operate, and we tell you which clauses are worth fighting for and which are not.

Services in detail

What we do, and what you get

Mergers, acquisitions and investments

Buy-side and sell-side support across share purchases, asset transfers and growth investment. We run the process from heads of terms to completion: due diligence on the legal position, negotiation of the transaction documents, shareholder and board approvals, and the conditions that actually have to be satisfied before signing.

Joint ventures and shareholder arrangements

Joint venture and shareholders' agreements, reserved matters, funding obligations, deadlock and exit provisions. These documents decide who controls a venture and what happens when it stops working, so we spend disproportionate effort on the exit provisions rather than the celebratory ones.

Commercial contracts and supply

Drafting and negotiation of supply, distribution, licence, services and framework agreements. We test each obligation against the technical specification behind it, and where a performance guarantee matters, we make sure it can be measured and enforced rather than merely promised.

Corporate governance and compliance

Articles of association, board and committee structures, delegated authorities, conflicts registers, and the policies and reporting that keep a growing organisation compliant. Delivered as working documents with a schedule of what must be reviewed and when.

Commercial dispute resolution

Negotiation and mediation first, arbitration and proceedings where the matter requires it. For contractual claims involving works, plant or performance, our engineers establish the technical facts and we pursue the legal consequences — one coordinated case rather than two advisers arguing past each other.

Restructuring, shareholder exits and insolvency

Reorganisations, share buy-backs, director and shareholder departures, and restructurings in the territory around insolvency. We advise on personal liability, preference claims and the practical steps that protect value when a business is under pressure.

Questions

Corporate & Commercial FAQs

Do you act for buyers or sellers?

Both, in separate matters and on opposite sides of the market, subject to a conflicts check. The perspective changes but the standard of diligence does not — we will tell you what we would flag if we sat on the other side of the table.

How long does a share purchase take?

A straightforward private share sale typically runs six to twelve weeks from heads of terms, driven more by diligence and approvals than by drafting. Asset transfers and cross-border structures take longer because local formalities are involved. We give you a dated plan at the outset.

Can you review a contract someone else drafted?

Yes, and it is often the most valuable thing a client can buy. We mark the document up against their commercial priorities and give a short note on which clauses are dangerous, which are merely inconvenient, and which are not worth the negotiating capital.

Will you advise on a dispute as well as the contract?

Ideally yes — it is easier to win a dispute with someone who wrote the contract and understands why each clause is there. Where we did not draft it, we will say so plainly and tell you what additional technical evidence you will need.
