About the practice

Engineering rigour, applied to legal problems

Naeem Ahmed is a law and engineering consultancy. We work on the matters where a purely legal answer or a purely technical answer would both be incomplete.

Who we are

A small firm by design

The practice was built on a simple observation: businesses rarely have a problem that is only a problem for lawyers or only a problem for engineers. A supplier agreement fails because the specification was wrong. A project overruns because the risk was never allocated in the contract. A deal is mispriced because nobody modelled the asset.

So we kept both disciplines under one roof. Clients get one team, one invoice and one person accountable for the whole answer â€” and they get advice that has survived contact with a site visit and a spreadsheet.

We act for established businesses, start-ups, investors, developers, contractors and public bodies. We are comfortable taking on the matters other firms decline because they sit outside a single practice group, and equally comfortable telling a client that a proposed course of action is not worth the risk.

We are not everything to everyone

We do not handle matters outside law and engineering, and we will say so early. Where a client needs a specialist in another field, we work alongside that specialist rather than attempting it ourselves.

What we believe

Four commitments we hold ourselves to

1

Say the difficult thing early

If the numbers do not work or the clause will not hold, you hear it at the start, not at the end.

2

Price the scope, not the hour

Fixed fees wherever the work can reasonably be defined in advance, so your budget holds.

3

Go and look

We visit sites, meet contractors and read the drawings. Opinions from a desk are worth less.

4

One team, one answer

Legal and engineering input sits in the same conversation, so nothing falls between two advisers.
