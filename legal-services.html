    Legal Services

    Commercial legal advice for businesses that build, buy, sell and operate. Drafted to work in practice, and read alongside the technical documents that give the obligations meaning.

    Overview

    Advice that survives contact with the real project

    A contract is only as good as the specification behind it and the people operating it. Our legal work is written with that in mind: we read the drawings, we understand the supply chain, and we ask the technical question before the clause is settled.

    We act for companies throughout their life cycle, from formation and first investment through governance, growth, restructuring and exit. The instruction is usually commercial first and legal second, and our advice is written for the person who has to make the decision rather than the person who has to defend it.

    Capabilities

    What the legal practice covers

    Most instructions draw on more than one area. We scope the work around the outcome you need rather than billing you for disconnected modules.

    Transactions & investment

    Share and asset purchases, founder and growth investment, joint ventures, and the shareholder arrangements that hold them together.

    Commercial contracts

    Supply, distribution, licence, services and framework agreements, with liability and termination provisions matched to the actual commercial exposure.

    Questions

    Common questions about instructing us

    How is a legal instruction priced?

    Wherever the work can reasonably be defined in advance, we quote a fixed fee against a written scope. Where the scope is genuinely open — a dispute, for example — we work to a capped estimate with a staged review so you can stop or redirect the work.

    Can you act for us and for a counterparty?

    Not on the same matter. We run a conflicts check before you send us anything substantive, and we will tell you confidentially and without obligation if there is a problem.

    Do you act outside your specialist jurisdictions?

    We regularly instruct local counsel in the relevant jurisdiction and coordinate their work as part of a single engagement. We will always tell you where local advice is required and why.

    Can legal and engineering work be combined?

    Yes, and it is usually the most cost-effective way to run the instruction. See Corporate & Commercial or Construction & Infrastructure for worked examples.

    What does an initial consultation cost?

    Nothing. The first discussion is free and carries no obligation. If a conflicts check clears, we will follow up with a written scope and fee proposal.
