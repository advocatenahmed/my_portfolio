    Engineering Services

    Independent technical judgement on the assets, systems and projects clients buy, build, operate or finance. Grounded in buildable, costable detail rather than optimistic appraisal.

    Overview

    Technical opinion you can act on

    Our engineers work for the client, not the contractor and not the manufacturer. That independence is the whole point: an appraisal written to avoid a difficult conclusion is worse than no appraisal at all.

    We work across feasibility, design, delivery, operation and handover. The output is a written finding that someone in a board meeting, a lender's credit committee or a court can read and understand, with the reasoning shown rather than asserted.

    Capabilities

    What the engineering practice covers

    We work at whatever depth the question requires — a two-hour site visit, a full appraisal, or several months of delivery oversight.

    Feasibility & appraisal

    Site and options appraisal, buildability review, programme realism, whole-life cost comparison and a clear recommendation.

    Design review & audit

    Independent review of design intent, specification adequacy, code compliance and constructability before or during construction.

    Technical due diligence

    Condition, capacity, remaining life and compliance review of plant and infrastructure for acquisition, refinancing or concession.

    Project & construction management

    Client-side management, progress and quality monitoring, cost control, and reporting to lenders, boards and investors.

    Claims & expert evidence

    Delay, disruption and valuation analysis, defect assessment, and expert reports prepared for negotiation or proceedings.

    Sustainability & compliance

    Energy and carbon assessment, environmental obligations, and the technical evidence needed for regulatory and lender requirements.

    Standards

    How our reports are built

    Every technical report follows the same discipline, whatever the size of the instruction.

    • Findings separated from opinion, clearly labelled
    • Assumptions stated and their effect quantified
    • Sources, calculations and site evidence shown
    • Uncertainty expressed as a range, not false precision
    • Conclusions traceable back to the evidence
