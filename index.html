What we do

Two disciplines, one point of accountability

Most failures on a project are not purely legal or purely technical. They sit in the seam between them — a contract that does not match the design, a design assumption nobody recorded, a variation nobody valued. We work across that seam so our clients do not have to coordinate two advisers who have never spoken.

Practice one

Legal Services

Business-focused legal advice, delivered by people who have read the technical documents and asked useful questions about them. We act for companies at formation, through growth, restructuring and exit.

  • Transactions, share purchases and investment

  • Corporate governance and regulatory compliance
  • Commercial dispute resolution

How we work

A clear route from first call to final deliverable

You will know what we are doing, what it costs and what we need from you at every stage. If the scope changes, we say so before the work does.

  1. 1

    Initial consultation

    A no-obligation discussion to understand the objective, the constraints and how urgent the matter is.

  2. 2

    Scoping and fixed fee

    A written scope of work with deliverables, a fixed fee where the scope allows, and a realistic timetable.

  3. 3

    Technical and legal assessment

    Our team reviews the documents, the site and the numbers, then reports findings and options in plain language.

  4. 4

    Delivery and handover

    We implement, negotiate, supervise or present as required, and leave you with working files and clear advice.
